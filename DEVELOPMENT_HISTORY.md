# Development history

This timeline preserves the source repository's recorded development sequence. It is a summary, not the public Git ancestry. Original operational snapshots are kept separate from this sanitized community release; the references below identify original commits and may not resolve in this repository. No dates or authors have been backdated for the community release.

The application began on 9 August 2026, with feature work through 14 August. Later commits update platform packages and authentication templates. Many original commit messages were generic, so this timeline does not invent explanations of those changes. Community preparation on 5 November adds the privacy, licensing, preview safeguards, and tests listed in RELEASE_NOTES.md.
