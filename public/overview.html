The Empathy Measure

An experimental open-source conversation and reflection tool about seven emotional skills. It includes adaptive interview prompts, fictional sample reports, a seven-axis chart, and local report history.

This community edition opens in preview mode. Assessment inference requires your own compatible backend and deliberate opt-in. No assessment record or result email is sent by the assessment flow. If you enable inference, answers and context are sent to your configured AI service, which has its own retention rules.

Scores are experimental model outputs, not validated psychological measurements. Reliability, age norms, cross-language equivalence, and comparability across sessions have not been established. Do not use this tool for diagnosis, school admissions, hiring, grading, or other consequential decisions.

Reports and unfinished sessions can remain in this browser's local storage. Shared devices need extra care. See the privacy page before enabling any live use, especially with children.

The Empathy Measure was created by Eric Gonzalez-Payne.
